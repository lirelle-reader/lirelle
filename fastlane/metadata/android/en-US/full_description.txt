Lirelle reads, shows and plays the books, comics and audiobooks of your own server — BookOrbit, or Komga for comics. It signs in to a server you or a friend runs; there is no store, no account to create with us, and nothing to buy.

<b>Books</b>
• EPUB, MOBI, AZW/AZW3 and FB2, paged or scrolled
• Bookmarks, highlights, notes and search
• A reader to your liking: light, sepia, dark and black themes; serif, sans-serif, a font made for dyslexic readers, or the book's own; text size, line spacing, margins, alignment and hyphenation

<b>Read & listen</b>
• Audiobooks with chapters, playback speed, a sleep timer and audio bookmarks
• Read-along: the sentence being read is highlighted in the book, for the books that carry it

<b>Comics and manga</b>
• CBZ, CBR, CB7, PDF and DjVu
• Fit to the page or the width, two pages side by side, left-to-right or right-to-left per series
• Webtoons: a vertical reading direction, where the pages scroll one after the other without a break
• Panel by panel: the panels are found on the device and shown one after the other

<b>Everywhere</b>
• Download to read and listen offline; your progress goes back to the server when the network does
• Reading history, and the progress made on your other devices
• One profile per server and account, kept apart, switched in two taps
• Layouts made for phones and tablets, in English and French

<b>Your data stays yours</b>
Lirelle has no ads, no analytics and no tracking: it only talks to the servers you add.

<b>Free software</b>
Lirelle is free software under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later: https://github.com/lirelle-reader/lirelle

Lirelle needs Android 14 or later, and a BookOrbit server (3.8.0 or later) or a Komga server.
