Nobody trusted the barometer, least of all the man who owned it. Marta kept the window open a hand's width, whatever the weather. The gulls returned at dusk, all of them this time, loud as a quarrel. The lamp turned once every nine seconds, as it had for sixty years. The sea that evening was the grey of a much-washed shirt.
